How this estimate works

Quantities use standard thumb rules per sq ft of built-up area, shown in the table so you can check them: concrete 0.045 cum, steel 4 kg, 125 mm brickwork 0.167 sqm, plaster 0.28 sqm, floor area 0.093 sqm. Work-item rates come from PWD SoR 2022 — RCC ৳16,192/cum, 125 mm brickwork ৳1,411/sqm, 12 mm plaster ৳324/sqm, 600×600 tiles ৳1,698/sqm, interior paint ৳265/sqm. Doors, windows, sanitary and electrical are composite market rates. Location multipliers follow the SoR regional columns (Dhaka 1.00 → Rajshahi 0.94).

Disclaimer: a planning estimate only — not a quotation. Verify rates with a current market quotation and a structural design before budgeting.
